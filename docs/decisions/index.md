# Decisions

Read the global decisions first (`node_modules/@sandergeraedts/harness/decisions/index.md`),
then this list, then only the records your work touches. How the registry works is in
[README.md](README.md). One line per record: `- [D-NNNN Title](file.md): one sentence`. A decision that a
global one replaced ends its line with `Replaced by G-NNNN.`

## Accepted

- [D-0001 This project follows the harness](D-0001-this-project-follows-the-harness.md): The harness is installed as a pinned dev dependency at v0.7.0; its rules and global decisions (G-NNNN) apply here unless a project decision overrules one, and decisions, questions and tasks live as files in docs/.
- [D-0002 The app is a TanStack Start app with server functions and a 3D shelf, not a static single-page app](D-0002-the-app-is-a-tanstack-start-app-with-server-functions.md): Overrules G-0017. The front end is React and TypeScript on TanStack Start with server functions and server routes, Three.js through react-three-fiber, Vitest, its own CSS design system and a local copy of the shelf in localStorage.
- [D-0003 The browser never talks to PocketBase: the app's server functions on Netlify are its only client](D-0003-the-browser-never-talks-to-pocketbase.md): Overrules G-0018. PocketBase on the NAS behind Funnel stays the backend, but only the app's server functions and the scheduled nightly function on Netlify call it, with POCKETBASE_URL set for production only; Funnel exposes only the paths those need.
- [D-0004 Sign-in is email and password through the app's server, with sign-up only for addresses on an allowlist on the NAS](D-0004-sign-in-is-email-and-password-through-the-apps-server.md): Accounts are PocketBase users with email and password; the app's server keeps the token in an httpOnly session cookie for 30 days; only addresses in SIGNUP_EMAILS on the NAS can sign up; each record is its owner's only.
- [D-0005 The backend is deployed by its own image workflow and Watchtower on a NAS app set up by hand; harness infra is not used](D-0005-the-backend-is-deployed-by-its-own-workflow-and-watchtower.md): Overrules G-0028. The PocketBase image is built from deploy/nas/ by the Backend image workflow and published as ghcr.io/sandergeraedts/reading-list-pocketbase; Watchtower on the NAS pulls :stable; harness infra up and down are never run here.
- [D-0006 A save can never wipe the shelf: the server merges every save into its copy and keeps a history](D-0006-a-save-can-never-wipe-the-shelf.md): Every shelf save is folded into the server's copy book by book (shelfMerge.ts), so a device can at worst add books, never lose them; PocketBase keeps earlier versions in an admin-only library_versions collection; the browser's pre-account shelf moves only into an account without a shelf and is never deleted.
- [D-0007 Storytel refreshes update only the reading; titles, page counts, merges and other details Hanna set stay hers](D-0007-storytel-refreshes-update-only-the-reading.md): The ↻ refresh and the nightly Storytel sync bring status, progress, the reading log and the listening time up to date, but never change a title, page count of a paper book, merge or other detail she set herself; the nightly sync never adds books.
- [D-0008 For a book she only listens to, a minute of listening counts as a page, everywhere in the app](D-0008-a-minute-of-listening-counts-as-a-page.md): An audiobook or Storytel book she doesn't also have on paper or as an e-book gets one page per minute of listening; that number sets its thickness on the shelf and counts in bingo and every other page-based figure.
- [D-0009 Duplicate books are merged by hand or from suggestions, keeping the best of both copies](D-0009-merging-duplicates-keeps-the-best-of-both.md): The book panel suggests likely duplicates and lets Hanna merge any two books; the book she merges into keeps its title, cover and details, the furthest status and progress and the most times read win, lists are combined, and a merged copy never comes back.
- [D-0010 A book can have several formats, and when the shelf is grouped by format the physical copy wins](D-0010-a-book-can-have-several-formats-and-paper-wins.md): How I have it is a list (e.g. Paperback and Storytel), stored as Book.formats; search finds a book under any of them, and a book stands in one group only, physical first.
- [D-0011 Every group on the shelf gets a wide book nook with a scene and a tall sign; no slim dividers and no series figures](D-0011-every-group-on-the-shelf-gets-a-wide-book-nook.md): When the shelf is arranged, each group stands behind a wide book nook with one of ten scenes matched to its books and a vertical sign with its name; the slim labelled dividers and the series figures were tried and taken out.
- [D-0012 Arrange by offers title, author, series, language, year, reading status, rating and spice; genre is a filter](D-0012-the-arrange-by-menu-and-genre-as-a-filter.md): Genre left Arrange by for a filter menu of its own, and colour, how I have it and date added left the menu at Hanna's request; they stay valid saved arrangements, so a shelf saved that way still loads.
- [D-0013 The app asks nothing in pop-ups: what it wants to know waits in Waiting for you on the dashboard](D-0013-no-pop-ups-questions-wait-on-the-dashboard.md): The rating question after finishing a book and Part of a series? are no longer dialogs; they wait in a Waiting for you block at the top of the dashboard, which hides itself when nothing is waiting.
- [D-0014 No explanatory hint texts under controls and blocks](D-0014-no-explanatory-hint-texts.md): Hanna asked, block by block and then in one pass, for the explanations under the shelf, the book panel, the dashboard blocks and Add book to go; error hints, the calendar's tap hint, sign-up and barcode texts and messages for empty blocks stay.
- [D-0015 No quotes anywhere in the app; quotes she already typed stay in her data](D-0015-no-quotes-in-the-app.md): The quote card, loved quotes and the quotes field in the book panel were removed at Hanna's request; Book.quotes and Book.lovedQuotes stay in the data, unused, so nothing she typed is lost.
- [D-0017 No AI anywhere: every feature works without an AI service or key](D-0017-no-ai-anywhere.md): The AI page-edge designer, AI recommendations and AI series lookup were removed on 12 November 2026 with the Anthropic SDK and ANTHROPIC_API_KEY; page edges are picked by hand or from the cover, and lookups use Open Library.
- [D-0018 No Google Books: search, ISBN lookups and imports use Open Library and Storytel only](D-0018-no-google-books.md): Google Books search and the Google Play Books import were built and reverted the same day, because both need a Google Cloud Console setup (an API key and an OAuth client) that isn't going to happen.
- [D-0019 No native app, so no home-screen widget](D-0019-no-native-app-or-home-screen-widget.md): Hanna's request for a scrollable phone widget of the shelf is not planned: a real home-screen widget needs a native app, and iPhone widgets can't scroll; the app stays a web app.
- [D-0020 The look is one design system: cosy library, one warm gold accent, a lavender striped wallpaper on every page, checked for contrast by a test](D-0020-the-look-one-design-system-gold-on-lavender.md): Colours, six text sizes, spacing, corners and a 44 px tap size are tokens at the top of styles.css, shown on the unlinked /style page; every page has the same lavender striped wallpaper with gold as the one accent, and colours.test.ts fails when text falls below WCAG contrast.
- [D-0021 New for you (suggested books not on the shelf) is on the dashboard again; Complete your series, Upcoming releases and Listening and reading stay off; books not out yet go on the shelf](D-0021-new-for-you-is-back-the-other-removed-blocks-stay-off.md): At Hanna's request in #186 a block of suggested books from Open Library that aren't on her shelf is back as New for you; the other three blocks removed in #123 stay off, a book whose publication date is still to come goes on the shelf as want-to-read, and What next? leaves it out.
- [D-0022 Hanna can make the room hers: lavender or midnight-blue wallpaper on every page, five woods, and which decorations stand where](D-0022-she-can-pick-the-wallpaper-wood-and-decorations.md): A 🎨 Room panel by the shelf sets the wallpaper (lavender stripes or midnight blue with gold stars, on every page), the bookcase wood (walnut, honey oak, white-washed, ebony, cherry) and the decorations (on/off, shelf order), per device; the colour check covers both wallpapers.
- [D-0023 Where a book is set is hers once she edits it: Open Library fills it once, never over her places](D-0023-places-she-sets-are-hers.md): Set in in the book panel shows a book's places; once Hanna adds or removes one, the list is hers and Open Library never looks it up or overwrites it again, also when she empties it; a made-up place she types counts as an imaginary land.
- [D-0024 The buying jar spends what a bought book cost: €5 is one coin, exact, and the jar may go below zero](D-0024-the-buying-jar-spends-what-a-book-cost.md): A bought book (paperback, hardcover or e-book) gets an optional price asked when it is added; each euro costs 1/5 coin from the 📚 buying jar, which shows only its coin count and can go below zero; no price costs nothing, and the 3-coin treat is gone.
- [D-0025 Every book suggested from her pile follows one rule: series strictly in order, nothing not out yet, no sleep reads, and re-reads only on bingo's re-read square](D-0025-suggestions-from-her-pile-follow-one-rule.md): Make a wish, What next?, the weekly dust-off book, bingo suggestions and A–Z tips for want-to-read books all come from readNextChoices, which now also leaves out sleep reads; the bingo re-read square is the only place a finished book is suggested.
- [D-0026 Where your books are set shows her covers, never numbers](D-0026-the-world-map-shows-her-covers-not-numbers.md): The world map block fills each country with the covers of the finished books set there and shows imaginary lands as rows of tiny covers; no counts appear anywhere in the block (map, tooltip, list, lands, screen-reader label or waiting line).
- [D-0027 A book's spine colour is only changed by her: nothing in the app recolours a book on its own](D-0027-a-spine-colour-is-only-changed-by-her.md): book.color stays the one stored colour, with no flag for a chosen one; the app never recolours an existing book automatically, and only her own actions change it: a swatch, Back to automatic, or a new cover she picks.
- [D-0028 Favourites keep the order she drags them into, apart from their sections, on every device, through the existing star and unstar route](D-0028-favourites-keep-her-order-on-every-device.md): In Arrange she can drag blocks within Favourites; the order is the order of layout.favourites, separate from each block's place in its own section, and it follows the account by sending a move as an unstar and re-star of the shortest tail, worked out against the account's list when it is sent, so the server on the NAS doesn't change.
- [D-0030 The want-to-read pile's height uses the thickness of real printed books, and audiobooks and e-books count as the printed book](D-0030-the-pile-height-uses-real-book-thickness.md): Each want-to-read book counts once, as a printed book: a paperback is 0.07 mm a page plus 1 mm of cover, a hardcover 0.07 mm a page plus 5 mm of boards; an e-book counts as a paperback, and a book she only listens to as the printed book, at 30 pages an hour of listening (minutes ÷ 2) when its minutes are known; a book with no length counts as 280 pages. Under the switch the Audiobook kind shows total listening time instead of a height. The 3D shelf keeps its own thickness.
- [D-0031 The pile's pace line follows the All · Paper · E-book · Audiobook switch, and audiobooks are counted in hours](D-0031-the-pace-line-follows-the-pile-switch.md): With a kind picked under the pile switch, the pace line counts only the waiting books of that kind, at the pace she finished books of that kind in the last year (the same finishes and window as the All pace, sleep reads and re-reads included, a book counting in every kind it has); for Audiobook it divides the waiting listening time by the hours of audiobooks finished a month; a kind with no finishes in the last year gets a short no-pace line; All stays as it is.
- [D-0032 Zoomed in, she can move along the shelf: ‹ › buttons, dragging the wall, the arrow keys and a one-finger swipe](D-0032-zoomed-in-she-can-move-along-the-shelf.md): When the shelf is zoomed in, round ‹ › buttons at its left and right edges move the view sideways on every device (hidden when not zoomed in, disabled at an end); on the computer she can also drag the empty wall with the mouse and use ← →, on the phone swipe with one finger; a press on a book still opens or moves the book, and the arrows are only the shelf's when nothing else has the keyboard.
- [D-0035 Books are hardcovers on every device again, drawn as one light piece on the shelf, with a Detailed books switch per device](D-0035-hardcovers-on-every-device-with-a-detailed-books-switch.md): Phones show the hardcovers (pages, boards, spine, ridges) again, as the computer does; on the shelf each hardcover is one merged piece of at most two draws, the full parts only for a book taken out; a Detailed books switch in the 🎨 room panel, kept per device and on by default, turns them into the simple blocks; the SafeBookshelf note stays.
- [D-0036 The Want-to-read pile block's grey line counts every audiobook's listening time, by the same rule as the 🎧 line](D-0036-the-pile-blocks-listening-time-counts-every-audiobook.md): In the Want-to-read pile block the grey “… pages · … to listen” line takes its listening time from every want-to-read book with Audiobook or Storytel (minutes, else pages × 2, else 560, as the 🎧 Audiobook line, D-0030), so both show the same hours; its pages stay the real page counts of the want-to-read books she doesn't only listen to, without estimates, so a paper book she also has on Storytel adds its pages and its minutes.
- [D-0037 Travel badges count the places of her finished books exactly as the world map does, each country on one continent (four on two), dated by the first finish of the book that earned them](D-0037-travel-badges-count-places-like-the-map.md): Countries, continents, imaginary lands and the Netherlands badge count the finished books the world map counts (status read or read at least once, places known, placesOf with the map's country names), the same land in other spellings once; each badge's day is the first finish day of the book that brought it; every map country belongs to one of six continents after Natural Earth, except Russia, Turkey, Kazakhstan and Egypt, which count for both continents they lie in, and the French Southern and Antarctic Lands count for none.
- [D-0039 Her birthday for the secret Birthday book badge is kept only on the device, as a day and a month, and never goes to her account or the server](D-0039-her-birthday-stays-on-the-device.md): The Badges block asks for a birthday (day and month, no year) once per device and keeps it in that browser's storage under hannas-reading-list:birthday as MM-DD; badges() takes it as an option and every place that works out badges passes it; it is never part of the shelf, the Book data, the account or anything sent to the server at Sander's home.
