# Context

- #83, #86 and #90 (2026-11-08): remove the explanations under Reading streak, What next? ("no
  re-reads…") and Books read. Done in #95.
- #130 (2026-11-13): she went through the instruction texts and picked which could go: the hints
  under the shelf and above an open book, the Arrange-mode text, the bingo explanation, the notes
  under empty dates, pages, length and Sleep read in the book panel, and the note under Published in
  Add book. She kept the calendar's "Tap a day to see what you read.", the sign-up and barcode
  texts, the ISBN and date error hints, and the messages for empty blocks.

# Decision

- The app shows **no explanatory hint texts** under controls, blocks or the shelf.
- **What stays:** error messages and hints that say how to fix a wrong entry, messages for an empty
  block, the calendar's tap hint, and the sign-up and barcode texts.

# Rationale

She knows the app and found the explanations clutter; she chose the few that help.

# Consequences

- New features explain themselves through labels and layout, not helper sentences.

# Guidance for agents

- Don't add helper or instruction text to a new block or field; an error message or an
  empty-state message is fine.
- When something seems unclear without a hint, ask Hanna rather than adding one.
